# Hanna and the Kettke Kingdoms

## Volume I of the Hanna Trilogy

*An extraordinary journey into a world beyond imagination.*

---

## The Story

Hanna's life is about to change.

Beyond the familiar world lies Kettke, a land of kingdoms, extraordinary creatures, ancient histories, and mysteries waiting to be uncovered.

What begins as an unexpected adventure will lead Hanna into a world unlike anything she has ever known.

Along the way, she will discover that courage can take many forms, that friendship can arise in the most unlikely places, and that some journeys change far more than the traveler.

But Kettke holds secrets.

And Hanna's arrival may be more important than she could ever imagine.

## About This Edition

**Hanna and the Kettke Kingdoms** is the official English title of the Spanish novel *Hanna y los Reinos de Kettke*.

This edition is written in natural American English while preserving the story, characters, dialogue, and narrative identity of the original work.

The Spanish edition remains the authoritative literary source.

Names, fictional cultures, places, and terminology belonging to the Kettke universe are maintained consistently throughout the translation.
