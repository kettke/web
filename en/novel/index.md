# The Hanna Trilogy

## One girl. An unknown world. An extraordinary destiny.

Hanna's story begins on Earth, but her journey will take her far beyond the world she knows.

In Kettke, she will encounter unfamiliar peoples, extraordinary creatures, ancient kingdoms, and a history whose secrets are closely connected to her own identity.

What begins as an adventure becomes a journey of discovery, friendship, courage, and transformation.

**Three novels. One extraordinary journey.**

---

## Volume I — Hanna and the Kettke Kingdoms

**The adventure begins.**

Hanna arrives in a world she knows nothing about.

As she explores Kettke, she discovers its inhabitants, its dangers, and the mysteries hidden within its history.

Along the way, she will forge friendships, face unexpected challenges, and begin to uncover the truth about her origins.

## Beyond the Trilogy

Hanna's story belongs to a larger narrative universe.

The kingdoms, peoples, creatures, languages, and legends of Kettke have histories of their own.

The **Atlas of Kettke** explores that world beyond the events of the novels, while future stories may take Hanna beyond Kettke itself.
