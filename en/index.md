# Welcome to Kettke

## The English Version

*One world. Many kingdoms. A story that crosses the boundaries between worlds.*

Welcome to the official English-language section of **Kettke**, the fantasy universe of **Hanna and the Kettke Kingdoms**.

This section is being developed to bring Hanna's story, the kingdoms of Kettke, and their inhabitants to English-speaking readers.

The original work was written in Spanish. This English edition aims to preserve its characters, narrative identity, and the richness of its fictional world.

---

## Explore the World of Kettke

Kettke is more than the setting of Hanna's adventures.

Its kingdoms, peoples, creatures, languages, and history form a larger fictional universe.

The official Atlas documents this world and its connections to the novels.

An English edition of the Atlas is planned.

## A Growing English Edition

The English section will gradually expand to include the novel, the Atlas, and other material from the Kettke universe.

The original Spanish text remains the authoritative literary source.

English translations will follow the established narrative canon, preserving the identity of the characters, places, and events.
